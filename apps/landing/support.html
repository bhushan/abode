Support

The handful of things that usually go wrong, and what to do about them.

Nothing happens when I press start

Open something with a video in it first. Abode needs a player on the page to build a link around. If the page is a service Abode does not ship support for, the popup will offer to turn it on for that site.

I cannot find the chat

It lives in the browser's side panel. Chrome has one; some Chromium browsers, Arc among them, do not, and on those Abode puts the same panel inside the page instead. Either way, "Open chat" in the popup brings it up.

We are in different rooms

Only one person presses start. Everybody else opens the link that person sends. If two people both press start, you get two rooms with nobody else in them.

Where are the play and pause buttons

There aren't any. Use the site's own player, exactly as you would alone, and the room follows. Abode never puts controls on top of somebody else's player.

My seeking keeps getting undone

The host has turned on host-only control, and the panel says so. Ask them to turn it off, or ask them to drive.

The panel says Offline

It reconnects on its own, backing off as it retries. If it stays offline, check that the relay is reachable: the popup shows a warning when it is not.

An episode change left me behind

The panel offers a "Catch up" button when the room moves on. It is an offer rather than an automatic jump, so a stray navigation never loses your place.

Something else

Open an issue. Include the browser, the site, and what you expected to happen. If it is a sync problem, whether the panel said "In sync" at the time is the single most useful detail.
