Privacy

There are no accounts, no analytics and no database. This page explains what that means concretely, and where you can check it for yourself.

What is collected

Nothing. Abode has no user records, no logins, no cookies of its own, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Nothing about you is stored anywhere, because there is nowhere to store it.

What stays on your machine

The display name and colour you pick, whether you are currently in a room, and that room's code. These live in the browser's own extension storage. Clearing the extension's data removes them. None of it is sent anywhere except the name and colour, which the other people in your room see, because that is what they are for.

What crosses the wire

While you are in a room: playback position, whether the video is playing, the playback speed, the page title of what is being watched, chat messages, and reactions. All of it goes to the relay and straight out to the other people in the same room. None of it is written down.

A room exists only while somebody is connected to it. When the last person leaves, it is gone, along with everything it was holding.

What the relay cannot see

The room code and the video address travel in the invite link's fragment, the part after the #. Browsers never transmit a fragment to a server, so the relay never receives it. It is not a policy commitment; it is where the data physically goes.

The relay does see the IP address any connection comes from, as every web server does, and it counts connections per address for a minute at a time to make guessing room codes expensive. That count is held in memory and never written down.

What the extension can reach

Abode asks for access to the services it ships support for, currently Netflix and Crunchyroll, and nothing else. If you want to sync some other site it asks you then, for that one site, and you can take the permission back in Chrome's settings at any time without reinstalling.

It reads the position of the video on the page and writes to it. It does not read page content, form fields, or anything else.

Checking any of this

The whole thing is open source under Apache-2.0, including the relay. If you would rather not take anyone's word for it, deploy your own relay and point the extension at it: the popup has a server setting for exactly that.
